## Overview

A creation of an overview Dashboard for a Brazilian E-Commerce firm

Questions Asked:
How are sales preforming?
Where are the most sales being made?
What products are the most profitable?
How has thing changed over time?

## Installation & Usage

- 1 Down load repository "Brazilian E-Commerce"
- 2 Open file "E-commerce.twb" in Tableau
- 3 Select the "Overview" tab
- 4 Adjust the "Date of Good's Shipped" Slider to change time slice for whole view (can take a few minutes)

- Alternatively Load the Adobe version called "E-Commerce.pdf"
    


## Visualizations 

3 KPI are displayed at the top (Total Sales value, Total Customers, No of Orders), along with a filter that changes the time the dashboard is for.
The lower displays give a more detailed brake down of the situation.
-  "Sales by State" lets a user see the relative value of the sales in each state. Tool tip gives details of each state.
- "Monthly Income" shows the value of the sales for each month and the general trend. 
- "Products" show the relative number of each product sold. The tool tip is needed to identify most products on the overview due to the scale. However the worksheet view shows them all clearly if more detail is needed
- "Price v's Quantity" shows the total revenue from a product & the number sold comparatively against other products. The Tool tip is needed to identify the name of each item.


## Analysis

While the demand for the products provided has been steadily growing in the two years the site has been operating (Have data for) & sales cover the whole of Brazil. There are a few notable trends in the data
- The vast majority of the sales have occurred in a few states. (AC, SP, MG, RJ)
- Some categories of goods are in high demand others are hardly any are sold. 
- While part of the issue for some of the goods not performing as well is that the range of goods has been steadily expanded over time. So it is not truly fair to compare goods that have been available from the start to ones that have just been added.


## Recommendations

- In order to improves probability it would be best to optimized the organization for the state where most sales occur (for cheaper delivery costs), while still covering the whole of Brazil.
 - A review of many poor performing products and a decision taken if any of them should be dropped, or other actions like advertising the products taken either now or after a year in the case of the newest products.
